# Privacy Policy: FoldShift and PaperVerse

Publisher: F7 Developer. Applies to *FoldShift: Origami Puzzle* (`com.f7developer.foldshift`) and
*PaperVerse: 3D Origami Studio* (`com.f7developer.paperverse`).

**We collect no personal data.**

- No account, sign-in or registration.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- No data is sent to any server. Both apps work fully offline.
- Game progress (FoldShift) and projects (PaperVerse) are stored only in the app's private storage on your
  device (`user://`). Uninstalling the app removes them.
- PaperVerse exports (FOLD, GLB, OBJ, PNG) are written only to a location you choose. Sharing them is your
  decision.
- The apps request no sensitive permissions. Haptic feedback uses the vibration setting of your device.

Children: the apps collect nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes: if this policy changes, the new version will be published with the app update.

Contact: via the F7 Developer contact listed on the Google Play store page.
